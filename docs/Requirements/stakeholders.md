## Stakeholders



### Customers
- **Role:** Sends/gives package to company
- **Interest:** Ensure company keeps proper records and delivers package successfully (Won't actually use system)
---

### Delivery Drivers
- **Role:** Deliver packages
- **Interest:** View assigned packages and update delivery status
---
### Recipients 
- **Role:** Receives the package
- **Interest:** Be able to contact company for relevant details regarding their package (delivery time estimate etc)
---
### Customer Service 
- **Role:** Assist customers + recipients 
- **Interest:** Search package information and give proper information to customers + recipients
---
### Warehouse Staff
- **Role:** Prepare and register packages 
- **Interest:** Be able to register information on packages on a centralized software.
---
### Dispatcher 
- **Role:** Assign packages to delivery drivers and manage delivery routes
- **Interest:** Have a centralized platform to efficiently manage driver assignments and generate/assign delivery routes
---

### Administrative Level
- **Role:** Review delivery metrics and overall company performance
- **Interest:** System access to company-wide metrics that provide insight on delivery performance. 
---
